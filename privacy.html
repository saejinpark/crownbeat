CrownBeat Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026 · Developer: dev.geppetto

CrownBeat does not collect any personal data. It has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and it does not connect to the internet.

Your best scores and settings (crown sensitivity, audio sync) are stored only on your Apple Watch, are never sent to the developer or any third party, and are removed when you delete the app.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
